8. WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED
==============================================================================
   * no lattice failure was observed on VALID film at any dose down
     to sigma 52, so the lattice threshold is a BOUND, not a
     measurement.
   * the crosstalk hypothesis (M21) is graded C and its one
     controlled test was underpowered: Fisher p = 1.000.
   * the crystallographic identity of the nano-domains is NOT
     claimed; this work measures orientation, not lattice vectors.
